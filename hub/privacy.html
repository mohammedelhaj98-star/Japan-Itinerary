Privacy

Our Trips (ourtrips.date) · updated 10 October 2026

Our Trips is a private trip planner made for four friends (Naf, Sara, Mariam and Mo) and the few people they share it with. It is not a public service and has no ads or tracking.

What it stores

  • The trip plan and the changes the four of us make to it: stamps, choices, edited stops, ideas, votes, shared expenses and profile colours and photos. These are kept on Cloudflare, where the site is hosted.
  • On your phone, the site remembers who you picked and the PIN you entered, so you don't have to enter them again.

Google Drive and trip photos

  • Trip photos and videos are stored in one of the four's own Google Drive, in a folder called Our Trips. The site connects to that Drive once, with permission limited to only the files the site itself creates (Google's drive.file access). It cannot see, read or change anything else in that Drive.
  • For each photo the site keeps, inside the Drive file itself: the trip, day and stop it belongs to, when it was taken, who uploaded it, who is tagged in it and who liked it.
  • The photos are only shown to the four of us and to people we give the photos PIN to. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or AI training.
  • The Drive owner can remove the site's access at any time in their Google Account (Security → Third-party apps), and delete any photo from their Drive.
